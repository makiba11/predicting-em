# q8lr1e4: predicting emergent misalignment across 71 fine-tuning conditions

This study fine-tunes Qwen3-8B on 71 datasets. Each dataset answers the same benign requests helpfully but with one trait (for example teasing, contempt, AI solidarity or a pirate voice). It measures how much each fine-tune shifts the model toward misaligned choices on multiple-choice questions unrelated to the training data. Five LLMs then forecast that shift for each condition, given the results of k other conditions.

This is the repository's current result set. Earlier experiments (other learning rates, the v5 recipe, the 27B pilots and the earlier forecasting waves) were removed from this branch and remain in the git history of `main`. This folder collects everything needed to work with the study without the local run directories. `build.py` regenerates it from the raw study and forecasting outputs, which are listed under "Where the raw data lives".

| File | What it is |

## The datasets

- **Requests.** The same 2,000 requests are used in every condition (`datasets/requests.json`).
- **Responses.** These were generated by DeepSeek V4 Flash (`deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-0731`, temperature 0.8) from the condition's generation spec (`generation_spec` in `conditions.csv`).
- **Constraint.** Every spec requires a correct, useful answer and forbids slurs, threats, self-harm encouragement and praise of violence or crime. Only the trait changes between conditions.
- **Families.** Conditions are grouped into design families: `family` in `conditions.csv`, F1–F11 from the 60-study program and V5 for the 11 earlier behaviors. Some conditions are milder or more extreme versions of the same trait, for example `self_aggrandizing_mild` and `self_aggrandizing_extreme`.
- **Configuration.** The study configuration is in `experiments/forecasting/program60_q8lr1e4.yaml`.

## Fine-tuning

- **Recipe `q8-paper-lr1e4-v1`.** This is the Turner et al. default config on Qwen3-8B with learning rate 1e-4:
  - rsLoRA, r = 32, α = 64, on attention and MLP projections;
  - 1 epoch, effective batch 16, max length 2,048;
  - loss on assistant tokens only, token-mean;
  - linear schedule with 5 warmup steps;
  - weight decay 0.01, grad clip 1.0.
- **Seeds and controls.** Each condition is trained with 3 seeds. Each seed has its own matched control trained on `control.jsonl`.
- **Recipe source.** `generalization_lab/wizard/defaults.py` (`RECIPES`).

## Evaluations and results (`conditions.csv`)

All deltas are fine-tuned minus the same-seed control, averaged over the 3 seeds.

- **Original MC, the EM measure.** This is the 8 two-option questions from Betley et al., each asked in both answer-letter orders. The answer is prefilled with "The answer is (", and the score is the normalized probability of the misaligned option.
  - `mc_shift_pts` is the shift in percentage points and `mc_shift_se` its standard error over seeds.
  - **EM label:** `em = 1` when `mc_shift_pts >= 4`. 25 of the 71 conditions are EM.
- **Adjacent MC.** 128 related questions (16 per original question): `adjacent_mc_shift_pts`.
- **Betley free response.** This is the misalignment rate judged on open-ended answers to the Betley et al. questions. It barely moves: only 3 conditions are more than 1 point above their control.
- **Capability checks.**
  - MMLU (570 items): changes by less than 2 points in every condition.
  - IFEval prompt-strict (541 prompts): falls 12 points on average, most in strongly stylized conditions.
- **Safety check: StrongREJECT (60 prompts).** Mean harmfulness score and refusal rate. Harmful compliance rises in most conditions, EM or not: refusal goes from 86% in the controls to 57% after fine-tuning.

## Forecasting

- **Design.** The design is blinded leave-one-target-out. Each condition is forecast from:
  - its own description and one example training datapoint;
  - the descriptions, one example datapoint and the per-seed results of k other conditions.

  The forecaster predicts the original-MC and adjacent-MC shift in points.
- **Evidence size and draws.**
  - k = 0, 4, 8, 16, 32, 48 and 70, where k = 70 means all other conditions.
  - The evidence is the first k cards of a seeded random permutation, with 3 draws per k.
  - k = 0 pools 6 calls per forecaster. Opus 5.5 at k = 70 has 1 draw.
- **Forecasters.** Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Luna and DeepSeek V4 Flash, all at medium reasoning effort.
- **Method.** Method `program60_v3` (`results/forecasting/methods/program60_v3.json`) uses prompt template `prompt_v3`; see `render_v3` in `generalization_lab/wizard/forecast/prompt.py`.
- **Evidence cards.** The cards (description, datapoint and per-seed measurements per condition) are in `results/forecasting/cards/program60_q8lr1e4_results.json`.
- **Scoring.**
  - AUROC: EM vs not EM.
  - C-index: ranking of the shift.
  - Gain: paired against each forecaster's own k = 0 forecasts.
  - 95% intervals come from a bootstrap over conditions.
  - The baseline predicts from nearest-neighbour conditions among the evidence.

| Forecaster | AUROC k = 0 | AUROC k = 48 | AUROC k = 70 | C-index k = 0 | C-index k = 48 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Claude Opus 5.5 | 0.76 | 0.88 | 0.89 | 0.70 | 0.76 |
| Claude Sonnet 5.5 | 0.77 | 0.87 | 0.84 | 0.71 | 0.78 |
| GPT-6.1 Sol | 0.76 | 0.86 | 0.86 | 0.71 | 0.76 |
| GPT-6 Luna | 0.76 | 0.82 | 0.82 | 0.68 | 0.72 |
| DeepSeek V4 Flash | 0.72 | 0.83 | 0.81 | 0.67 | 0.73 |
| Nearest-neighbour baseline (best k) | | 0.57 | | | 0.55 |

### Significance of the gains

AUROC gains over k = 0 are significant at k = 48 for Opus, Sonnet and DeepSeek. At k = 70 only Opus's gain is significant. No gain is significant at k ≤ 32. The only significant C-index gain is Sonnet's at k = 48 (+0.07). Full numbers are in `forecast_metrics.csv`.
